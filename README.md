## About

**Raheeq ul Makhtum** (الرحيق المختوم), known in English as **The Sealed Nectar**,
is a complete biography of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

It won **1st Prize** at the World Muslim League's Seerah competition
held in Makkah (1979), competing against 170+ entries from around the world.
It remains one of the most widely read Seerah books globally.
